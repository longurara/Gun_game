# Airplane

**Airplane** by **Poly by Google**, from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/2eG17I-VDiG), licensed under **[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)**.

The original GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime changes: aligns its nose to +Z, scales the aircraft to a 34 m wingspan, adapts materials for LASTLIGHT's lighting and adds navigation lights. Attribution, source, license and modifications also appear in the game's asset credits.

Source URL, byte count and SHA-256 are recorded in `manifest.json`. Reproduce acquisition with `npm run assets:fetch-aircraft`. Verified 2026-10-06.
