https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They may be used, modified and distributed in personal and commercial projects. Attribution is not required; this project retains the source and creator information as a courtesy.

- Ultimate Guns Pack: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Guns-Pack-cpgUfI4t2F
- Ultimate Modular Men Pack (SWAT): https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Modular-Men-Pack-ZiH8muWqwQ
- Per-file source pages, original download URLs, file sizes and SHA-256 checksums: `manifest.json`.

These are the authored, unmodified GLB downloads. LASTLIGHT normalizes gun orientation/scale and adapts lighting at runtime. The SWAT animation rig is cloned per player, with procedural hand/leg IK for weapon grips and crouching. Only the default outfit uses this character; bots and unlocked outfits retain the procedural character.

The separate Kenney UI assets and their original license are in `src/assets/ui/kenney/`.
