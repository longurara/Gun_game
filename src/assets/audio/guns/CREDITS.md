# Gunfire sound credits

- **Snake's Authentic Gun Sounds** — SnakeF8 / F8 Studios. [Original pack](https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-authentic-gun-sounds). The author grants free commercial use and optional attribution; this first pack is not labelled CC0 here.
- **Snake's SECOND Authentic Gun Sounds Pack** — SnakeF8 / F8 Studios. [Original pack](https://f8studios.itch.io/snakes-second-authentic-gun-sounds-pack). Public domain / CC0, clarified by the author in the license discussion on that page. Original ReadMe is retained.
- **Light Machine Gun** — KuraiWolf. [Original sound](https://opengameart.org/content/light-machine-gun), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original filename: `lmg_fire01.mp3`. License text is retained in `CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

All eight clips were converted to mono, trimmed, peak normalized, faded and encoded as OGG Vorbis. Exact source entries, processing and SHA-256 hashes are in `manifest.json`. Two full SnakeF8 archives and source pages are kept under ignored `output/assets/gun-audio/` by `npm run assets:fetch-gun-audio`.

These are shared sound palettes, not exact recordings of every weapon model. Shotguns use a 20 gauge recording; suppressed guns use a quieter .22LR recording with an additional low-pass filter, as suggested by the original author. Heavy anti-materiel rifles, bows and launchers retain synthesized sound effects.

Verified 2026-10-06. The permission sentence on both SnakeF8 pages reads: “All sounds can be used commercially, and credit is not needed”.
